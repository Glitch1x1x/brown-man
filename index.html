Every animal has its own special skills. Pick a card to learn a fun fact!

MAMMAL · GRASSLAND

Lion

Lions are big cats that live in groups called prides.

A lion's roar can be heard from several kilometres away.

MAMMAL · SAVANNA

Elephant

Elephants use their trunks to smell, drink, and pick things up.

Elephants communicate with sounds that can travel long distances.

MAMMAL · OCEAN

Dolphin

Dolphins are clever ocean mammals that breathe air.

Dolphins use clicks and whistles to communicate with one another.

BIRD · TROPICAL FOREST

Parrot

Parrots use strong beaks to crack seeds and nuts.

Some parrots can copy sounds they hear around them.
